Akima Operator Integral (Proposed)

ARC-VLA (Ours)

Reconstructs continuous actions via Akima tangent interpolation over the cumulative displacement integral. Enforces discrete block-sum conservation ($\sum_{\tau=0}^{k-1} v_{ik+\tau} = S_i$) and eliminates sparse-knot trajectory overshoot.

C2-Continuous Polynomial

Cubic Spline

Standard piecewise cubic polynomial through knot positions. Highly competitive under mild decimation ($2\times$), but derivative unboundedness induces significant overshoot during sharp directional reversals at $4\times$ and $8\times$.

Basis Spline Regularization

B-Spline (ε)

Parametric B-spline with curvature penalty $\epsilon$. Because control points do not interpolate knot positions directly, it suffers severe corner-cutting and trajectory shrinkage without saturation clipping repairs.

Scientific Takeaways & Reviewer Takeaways

Mechanistic Explanation for Rate Conversion Sensitivity Across Regimes
Discussion

1. Akima Tangents Suppress Decimation Overshoot (Push-T)

On Push-T at $4\times$ decimation ($k=4$, 2.5 Hz), ARC-VLA exhibits an empirical lead of +18.0 pp over Cubic Spline ($72.0\%$ vs. $54.0\%$). Under paired McNemar testing at $n=50$, this difference does not reach statistical significance ($p = 0.108$), but the directional mechanism is consistent: Akima tangents locally weight adjacent slopes to suppress polynomial oscillations between sparse knots, preventing premature tool detachment.

2. High-Frequency Upsampling Crossover (20 Hz & 40 Hz Measured)

In live evaluations at 20 Hz and 40 Hz ($n=10$ paired), dense knot spacing alters the performance hierarchy. At 20 Hz, ARC-VLA and Cubic Spline tie at 80.0% (Spline achieving 0.999 peak coverage). At 40 Hz, B-Spline leads at 80.0% (0.996 coverage) via continuous curvature damping, while Cubic Spline reaches 70.0% and ARC-VLA scores 60.0%.

3. RoboMimic Invariance Across Rates (91.8% 3-Way Tie)

Across all nine evaluated RoboMimic cells (Lift, Can, Square at $2\times, 4\times, 8\times$; $n=100$ paired), ARC-VLA, Cubic Spline, and B-Spline each average exactly 91.8%. At $8\times$ decimation, all three methods tie identically: Lift 99%, Can 80%, Square 79%. In low-acceleration teleoperation regimes, the choice of action rate interpolant is neutral.

4. Continuous IoU vs. Thresholded Cliff Effects

In Push-T, continuous coverage degrades gracefully ($0.963 \to 0.954 \to 0.804$ for Cubic Spline; $0.938 \to 0.961 \to 0.843$ for ARC-VLA). However, the binary success threshold at $\text{IoU} \ge 0.95$ amplifies sub-millimeter trajectory deviations into dramatic cliff drops. Reporting both continuous IoU and binary success is essential to avoid misdiagnosing threshold artifacts.
