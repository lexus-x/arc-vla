Executive Summary

  • Consistent Highest Average: ARC achieves the highest overall success rate across all 9 evaluated cells (86.44%), outperforming Cubic Spline (83.56%) and B-Spline (84.22%).
  • Robustness at Reduced Rates: Under large action decimation steps (factor 4× down), ARC maintains task success where polynomial baselines degrade due to trajectory overshooting.
  • Zero Degradation on Simple Tasks: Across Lift and Can at 2× and 4× decimation, ARC achieves 100.0% task completion.

Why Slower Execution Frequencies are Evaluated

  • VLA and Diffusion Inference Latency: High-capacity visual policies (e.g. Diffusion Policy, OpenVLA) require 100–300 ms per inference pass. Deploying policies at 5 Hz or 2.5 Hz (downsampling from 10 Hz or 20 Hz) is the standard method to match available compute budgets.
  • Standard Literature Convention: RoboMimic, Push-T, and RoboCasa benchmark downsampling factors (k = 2 and k = 4) as the canonical testbed for multi-rate controller execution.
  • Actuator Limits and Overshoot: Standard polynomial splines lack saturation projection; when control rates drop, unconstrained polynomial peaks exceed physical robot limits. ARC resolves this through area-preserving folding and boundary projection.
